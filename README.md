## 📖 Sobre el proyecto

**Faralid K-Music** es un reproductor de música para Android pensado para quienes 
quieren escuchar su biblioteca local sin que nadie los esté vigilando, sin anuncios 
que interrumpan y sin funciones "premium" que escondan lo básico.

Sin cuentas, sin suscripciones, sin letra pequeña. Solo tu música.

### Por qué existe

- **Privacidad real.** Cero telemetría. Cero analytics. Cero llamadas HTTP a servidores. 
  La app no tiene cliente de red, así que no puede enviar nada aunque quisiera.
- **Sin anuncios.** Ni banners, ni intersticiales, ni recompensados. Nunca.
- **Sin permisos innecesarios.** Solo lo estrictamente necesario para leer tu música.
- **Código abierto.** Puedes auditar exactamente lo que hace y cómo lo hace.

## 📖 About the project

**Faralid K-Music** is an Android music player designed for those who want to
listen to their local music library without being monitored, without intrusive
ads, and without "premium" features locking away basic functionality.

No accounts, no subscriptions, no fine print. Just your music.

### Why it exists

- **True privacy.** Zero telemetry. Zero analytics. Zero HTTP requests to external servers. 
The app has no network client, so it couldn't send data even if it wanted to.
- **No ads.** No banners, no interstitials, no rewarded ads. Ever.
- **No unnecessary permissions.** Only what is strictly required to access your music.
- **Open source.** You can audit exactly what it does and how it does it. ---

## ✨ Features

### Playback
- 🎵 **Folder-based playback** — browse your directories exactly as you’ve organized them
- 📀 Playback by artist, album, or song
- 🔁 Play queue with "play next" and "add to end" options
- 🎚️ Basic equalizer with presets
- ⏱️ **Resume position** — pick up exactly where you left off in a podcast or audiobook

### Interface
- 🎨 **Dynamic colors** extracted from album art (Material You)
- 🌗 Automatic light/dark mode
- 🎧 **Sleep timer** — pause after 5, 15, 30, or 60 minutes, or at the end of the current track
- 📱 Minimalist widget

### System Integration
- 🔌 **Automatic pause when headphones are disconnected**
- 🔗 **Optional auto-resume when connecting via Bluetooth**
- 📢 Notification controls with support for Bluetooth headphones

### Privacy
- 🚫 No ads
- 🚫 No telemetry
- 🚫 No user accounts
- 🚫 No internet connection required
